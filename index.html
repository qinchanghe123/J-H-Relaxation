Treatment-Oriented Relaxation

Relieve Tension, Reset the Body, Calm the Mind

Head therapy massage works through the scalp, temples, forehead, jaw area, base of the skull, neck, and shoulders. This style is especially helpful for guests who feel heavy-headed, mentally tired, tight around the neck, or tense after long hours at a computer.

Therapeutic massage uses slow compression, kneading, acupressure-style holds, assisted stretching, and focused pressure to address tight muscles and restricted comfort zones. The goal is to help your body feel more open, balanced, and comfortable.

Head Therapy Massage

Focus Areas: scalp · temples · jaw · neck · shoulders

Head therapy massage is designed for guests who carry stress in the head, neck, and shoulder area. The session may include gentle scalp work, temple pressure, neck release, shoulder compression, and slow calming massage around the upper back.

This service is especially suitable for office workers, drivers, students, guests with screen-time fatigue, and anyone who feels tightness around the base of the skull or shoulders.

Potential Benefits

  • Helps soften scalp, neck, and shoulder tension
  • Promotes deep relaxation and mental calm
  • May reduce the feeling of heaviness around the head
  • Supports better comfort after long screen time
  • Encourages easier breathing and a lighter upper body feeling
Please call for current pricing

Therapeutic Massage

Focus Areas: neck · back · shoulders · arms · legs

Therapeutic massage is a focused treatment-style session for guests who want targeted work on muscle tightness, daily aches, posture-related tension, and areas that feel stiff or overused.

The session can include firm but controlled pressure, compression through clothing, stretching, kneading, and slow release work. Pressure is always adjusted to your comfort.

Potential Benefits

  • Helps loosen tight muscles and soft tissue tension
  • Supports relaxation for the nervous system
  • May improve comfort in the neck, shoulders, back, and legs
  • Helps reduce stress-related body tightness
  • Promotes a refreshed, lighter, more mobile feeling after the session
Please call for current pricing

Fully Clothed Therapeutic Care

Massage staff and clients remain appropriately clothed during services. Please wear comfortable clothing that allows gentle movement and pressure work. If you have an injury, medical condition, recent surgery, or severe pain, please consult your healthcare provider before booking.
